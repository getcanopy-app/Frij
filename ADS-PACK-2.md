# Frij — Ad Pack v2: 10 repeatable scripts

Pack v1 (ADS.md) was the launch: five one-off ideas. This pack is for the
grind after launch: **1–2 posts a day for 60 days.** So every script here is a
*series*: once the format exists, a new episode is just a new fridge, a new
person or a new number. No new ideas needed.

Same rules as v1: vertical 9:16 · hook works with sound OFF inside 1.5s ·
captions burned in · under 25s · real, messy fridges only · first-person
solo-dev voice, no ad-speak · numbers beat adjectives.

**CTA line for every end card:** "Frij — cook what you have. Free on the
App Store." Once the 7-day trial is live, the caption can add "first week of
Frij+ is free."

---

## 1. Rate My Fridge — the street series
*TikTok + Reels. The highest-volume format: one episode per person you know.*

- **Hook:** text **"Letting an AI rate my friend's fridge"** + the friend
  standing guard in front of it, arms crossed.
- **0–4s:** friend reluctantly opens it. Quick pan.
- **4–9s:** scan, then the Roast card full screen. Hold on the score.
- **9–15s:** friend's genuine reaction (this IS the video. Don't script it).
- **15–20s:** "but it still found…" then the 3 dinners. End card.
- **Episodes:** roommate, mom, dad, gym-bro friend, the friend who only has
  sauces. Title each "Ep. 4: my mom's fridge."

## 2. $0 Dinner Challenge
*TikTok + Shorts. The money angle, as a repeatable challenge.*

- **Hook:** text **"Day 1 of spending $0 on dinner until my fridge is
  empty"** over the full fridge.
- **0–5s:** scan → pick one of the 3 dinners.
- **5–15s:** CapCut-speed cooking montage, then the plate.
- **15–20s:** text **"Saved: $18 (what DoorDash would've charged)"** plus a
  running total that grows each day.
- **Why it works:** a running counter makes people follow the account to see
  how far it goes. End the series with "the fridge is empty, I saved $___."

## 3. Use It Before It Dies
*Reels + TikTok. Sells the "use it up" feature through food-waste guilt.*

- **Hook:** close-up of sad wilting spinach. Text **"this spinach has 24
  hours to live"**.
- **0–5s:** open the Pantry tab: the spinach sits under **USE SOON**.
- **5–12s:** tap **Cook these** → dinners built AROUND the spinach. Text
  **"saved it."**
- **12–18s:** the finished plate. Text **"Nothing went in the trash this
  week."**
- **Episodes:** a different dying ingredient each time (half an onion, the
  last 2 eggs, the cilantro you bought for one recipe).

## 4. Reply-to-Comment: "Do mine"
*TikTok only. Turns every comment into the next video. Free content forever.*

- **Hook:** TikTok's reply-to-comment sticker: **"@user: bet it can't do
  anything with what's in MY fridge"** + their list (eggs, ketchup, tortillas,
  string cheese).
- **0–6s:** type or say their exact list into Frij.
- **6–14s:** the 3 dinners appear. Read the best one out loud, deadpan.
- **14–18s:** text **"Drop your fridge below 👇"**. That line generates the
  next episode.
- **Seed it:** if no comments yet, ask friends to comment the weirdest lists.

## 5. Dessert From Nothing
*TikTok + Reels. Dessert mode. The "wait, what?" angle.*

- **Hook:** text **"It's 11pm and I need something sweet. I have: flour,
  1 egg, peanut butter."**
- **0–5s:** switch Frij to Desserts → scan the pantry shelf.
- **5–15s:** pick one; quick bake montage.
- **15–20s:** bite + reaction. Text **"from 'nothing'."**
- **Episodes:** smoothies at 7am, snacks at 2am. Each mode is its own run.

## 6. Grocery Haul → 7 Dinners
*Reels + Shorts. Planning content performs well with the meal-prep crowd.*

- **Hook:** groceries dumped on the counter. Text **"I spent $54. How many
  dinners is that?"**
- **0–8s:** unload while saying items out loud, chips filling in LIVE (the
  voice feature without making it "about" voice).
- **8–15s:** generate; scroll the ideas. Text counter **"Dinner 1… 2… 3…
  7."**
- **15–20s:** text **"$7.71 a dinner."** End card.

## 7. Saved Reel → Actually Cooked It
*Reels first. A follow-up to v1's Reel-to-Real, ending on the payoff.*

- **Hook:** split screen: left = a viral food reel; right = blank. Text
  **"Cooking a saved reel for the first time ever."**
- **0–5s:** share the reel to Frij → it lands with the creator's thumbnail.
- **5–15s:** cook it; right side fills with your version (it can be
  imperfect. That's funnier).
- **15–20s:** side-by-side final plates. Text **"expectation vs. me."**
- **Bonus:** tag the original creator. Sometimes they duet, which gives you free reach.

## 8. The Streak
*TikTok. Build-in-public meets habit content. Low effort, daily.*

- **Hook:** text **"Day 12 of cooking every night instead of ordering"**
  over the Frij streak flame.
- **0–10s:** 3–4 second clips: tonight's scan → tonight's plate.
- **10–15s:** text **"DoorDash this month: $0 (last month: $380)."**
- **Why it works:** it's a diary, so people come back daily. Use the same
  opening shot every day so it's recognizable in 0.5s.

## 9. Founder Reacts to 1-Star Fears
*TikTok + Shorts. Founder voice; builds trust and handles objections.*

- **Hook:** you to camera, holding phone: **"Things people assume my app
  can't do"**.
- **0–15s:** rapid-fire, each one disproved on screen in 3s:
  "it can't read my messy fridge" → scan your worst shelf. "It only knows
  pasta" → dessert/smoothie modes. "I'll need to buy stuff" → it shows what
  you already have.
- **15–20s:** "I'm one person building this. Tell me what to fix." End card.
- **Episodes:** reply to real comments/reviews as they come in.

## 10. Rich Fridge vs. Broke Fridge
*TikTok + Reels. The contrast format; very shareable.*

- **Hook:** split screen, two fridges: one stocked, one bare. Text **"Same
  app. Two very different fridges."**
- **0–8s:** scan both.
- **8–16s:** both get 3 dinners. Linger on how good the broke fridge's are.
  Text **"broke fridge ate better."**
- **16–20s:** end card.
- **Episodes:** college vs. parents', vegetarian vs. carnivore, before vs.
  after grocery day.

## 60-day posting cadence

- **Every day:** 1 post minimum, at the same time (6–7pm your viewers' time,
  peak "what's for dinner" scroll). Post the same video to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- **Week 1–2:** rotate through all 10 formats once. That's the test.
- **Week 3+:** check which 2–3 formats got the most *saves and shares* (not
  likes) and give them ~70% of posts. Drop formats that flop twice.
- **Weekly:** remake the best hook from the week with a new opening shot.
  Same idea, new first 1.5s often beats the original.
- **When something hits:** post the next episode within 24h while the
  algorithm is still pushing you.
- **Track it:** in the backend `client:paywall_view` and `client:subscribe`
  events, look for jumps the day after a video pops. That's what tells you
  which formats sell, not just which get views.

## Creators (once 2–3 formats are proven)

Pay 3–5 small food or budget creators (10k–100k followers) $50–150 each to
film YOUR best-performing format in their own kitchen. Give each one a creator
code (the app already supports them) so you can see exactly who drove
installs.
