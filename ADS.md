# Frij — Launch Ad Pack v1

Grounded in the growth notes in VISION.md: positioning = "the 10-second cure
for decision fatigue in your kitchen," wedge = 21–30, high TikTok use, takeout
guilt. All five scripts are filmable with one phone, one fridge, zero budget.

**Golden rule:** nothing posts with a download CTA until 1.0 is LIVE. Film
everything now; fire on launch day. (A no-CTA teaser — script 1 without the
link — may post 1–2 days early as build-in-public bait.)

**Format rules for every cut:** vertical 9:16 · hook inside the first 1.5
seconds · captions burned in (most watch muted) · under 20 seconds · end card
= app icon + "Frij — cook what you have" + App Store badge.

---

## 1. The $400 Confession — THE launch video (founder-voice)
*Platform: TikTok + Reels + Shorts. Also the top asset for the Reddit posts.*

- **Hook (0–1.5s):** on-screen text over a chaotic open fridge:
  **"I was spending $400/month on DoorDash out of pure laziness."**
- **0–4s:** slow handheld pan across the real, messy fridge. No cleanup. The
  mess is the relatability.
- **4–8s:** "So I built this." Screen-record: tap Scan → camera sweeps the
  fridge → chips populate (eggs, steak, heavy cream…).
- **8–14s:** tap **Get 3 dinners** → the three cards land. Freeze a beat on
  the hero: *Garlic Butter Steak Bites with Parsley Rice*. Text: **"3 dinners.
  From food I already had. 10 seconds."**
- **14–18s:** end card. VO or text: "It's called Frij. Link in bio."
- **Sound:** trending calm-but-punchy audio, or raw room tone + one VO line.
- **Why it works:** founder authenticity + a number ($400) + a live demo that
  is genuinely impressive. This is the "show don't tell" the product was
  built for.

## 2. The Fridge Roast — the shareable one
*Platform: TikTok/Reels. Built to make VIEWERS want their own roast.*

- **Hook:** text: **"An AI just rated my fridge."** POV: nervous face.
- **0–5s:** scan the fridge (speed-ramp the scan).
- **5–12s:** cut to the Roast card filling the screen: **"4/10. Mostly
  condiments and commitment issues. Frij still found 3 dinners."** React
  genuinely (laugh/offended).
- **12–17s:** show the 3 dinners it found anyway. Text: **"…okay fine."**
- **CTA:** "Roast your own fridge — Frij, link in bio."
- **Series potential:** roast a friend's fridge, your mom's fridge, a
  roommate's. Each is a new video with zero new ideas needed.

## 3. Reel-to-Real — the import demo (ReciMe-lane)
*Platform: Reels first (native behavior), then TikTok.*

- **Hook:** text: **"You have 400 saved recipes you've never cooked."**
  over a screen-record scrolling an IG saved-food folder.
- **0–5s:** land on a real food reel (steak pasta type). Tap Share → **Frij**
  in the share sheet.
- **5–10s:** the "Sent to Frij ✓" card pops → cut to Frij: the recipe is
  there, **with the creator's actual video thumbnail**.
- **10–16s:** open it: **"You have 5 of the 7 ingredients."** Text: **"It
  checks your fridge. Automatically."**
- **CTA:** "Stop bookmarking. Start cooking. — Frij"
- **Why it works:** attacks the single most universal food-content behavior
  (saving reels into a void) with the one thing binder apps can't do — the
  pantry match.

## 4. The 6PM Standoff — the relatable skit
*Platform: TikTok. Lowest production, highest volume — this one can be
remade weekly.*

- **Hook (acted):** open fridge, stare, close it. Text: **"there's nothing
  to eat."** Open it again 2 seconds later. Stare. (The universal ritual.)
- **5–10s:** friend/roommate voice or text: "you literally have steak in
  there." → phone out → scan.
- **10–16s:** 3 dinners appear from the "nothing." Deadpan text: **"there
  was, in fact, something to eat."**
- **CTA:** end card.
- **Why it works:** pure recognition comedy. The product is the punchline.

## 5. Talk To Your Fridge — the voice flex
*Platform: Shorts + TikTok tech/AI audiences.*

- **Hook:** text: **"My grocery app has a keyboard. Mine has ears."**
- **0–10s:** unloading groceries, phone propped up, mic pill on: speak
  naturally — "eggs, heavy cream, parmesan, uh… tortillas, some cilantro" —
  while the chips populate LIVE on screen, waveform dancing.
- **10–15s:** pan to the filled pantry list. Text: **"20 items. Zero
  typing."**
- **CTA:** end card.

## Posting plan (launch week)
1. **Day −1:** script 1 *teaser cut* (no CTA) + "launching tomorrow" — post
   to personal accounts, r/SideProject-style build-in-public framing.
2. **Day 0 (approval day):** script 1 full cut with link. Same day: the
   Reddit posts (r/eatcheapandhealthy, r/mealprepsunday, r/apps) — NOT the
   ad; a text post in solo-dev voice ("I built an app that turns a photo of
   your fridge into 3 dinners — free, no ads, would love feedback") with the
   video attached.
3. **Day 1–2:** script 2 (Roast).
4. **Day 3–4:** script 3 (Import).
5. **Day 5+:** scripts 4–5, then remix winners. Volume beats polish; post
   daily variants of whatever performs.

## Voice rules (all captions/copy)
- First person, solo-dev honesty. Never ad-speak ("revolutionary",
  "game-changing" = banned).
- Numbers over adjectives ("3 dinners, 10 seconds, $0" beats "fast & easy").
- The fridge is always REAL and messy. A staged fridge kills the entire
  premise.
